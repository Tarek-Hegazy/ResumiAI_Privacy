Resumi AI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Resumi AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, stores and uses information while using the app.

Information We Collect

  • Your resume information that you enter inside the application.
  • Your language and application preferences.
  • Advertising identifiers required for Google AdMob rewarded and banner ads.
  • Anonymous diagnostic information that may be provided by Google Play services.

How We Use Your Information

  • Generate and improve resume content using AI features.
  • Provide application functionality.
  • Display rewarded and banner advertisements.
  • Improve stability and overall user experience.

Data Storage

  • Your resumes are primarily stored locally on your device.
  • AI requests are processed only when you choose to use AI features.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

  • This application uses Google AdMob.
  • Google may collect advertising identifiers according to its own Privacy Policy.
